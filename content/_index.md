We invite you to join us for the upcoming **2026 Vienna Soft Matter Day**, which will be a day-long event held on **20 November 2026** at the **Moonstone Seminar Center**, Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), 3400 Klosterneuburg. 

[You can download the **program** for the day here.](/program2026.pdf). For those of you with posters: you can view your poster number at the end of the program. 

The VSMD brings together soft matter experimentalists and theorists from across the greater Vienna area to share new results, exchange ideas, and form new collaborations.
It is also an open setting for young researchers to showcase their work and connect with scientists at every career level.

There will be the opportunity for PhD students and postdocs to present their work via talks, posters, and flash talks, with enough time for discussions throughout the day.


Our goal is to strengthen the local soft matter community and to create a welcoming space for scientific exchange. This is why the meeting is free of registration fees.
We encourage members of the soft matter community at all levels (PIs, postdocs, PhD students, master students, and anyone interested in soft matter research) to participate.

**Lunch & Coffee**: Lunch, coffee, and snacks will be provided free of charge.

The **venue** can be found on the [ISTA map](https://ist.ac.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ISTA_Campus-Map_03-2026_Legende_1.pdf). The Moonstone Building / Seminar Center is marked as building 24.


**Getting to ISTA with the Free VSMD Shuttle**

We have reserved a special shuttle to bring non-ISTA participants to the institute.  The shuttle will depart from near Schwedenplatz in Vienna [(map location here)](https://www.google.com/maps/d/u/0/viewer?mid=1e1k2E-PUX26yNKRk-8jVOQoWAqimH58&ll=48.21207234996443%2C16.37888788906717&z=17) at 08:30 on Nov 20.  A representative from ISTA will be there to let you know you are in the right place. We recommend to arrive early so you do not miss the shuttle. 

A shuttle is also reserved to return non-ISTA participants to Vienna (departure 19:00 after wine and cheese).


**Getting to ISTA by Public Transit**

You can also reach ISTA by public transit. [VOR AnachB](https://anachb.vor.at/#/explore?zoom=11.471221338178513&lng=16.38499999999999&lat=48.2071382489373) will help you find a suitable route, using "Institute of Science and Technology" as the destination.  Of course public transit can also be used to return to Vienna.

**Getting to ISTA by Car**

If you plan to arrive by car, you can drive directly to the [ISTA parking garage](https://www.google.com/maps/place/ISTA+Parking+garage/@48.3108494,16.2581506,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x476d0dd87a96850b:0xf11434c122747edf!8m2!3d48.3108494!4d16.2581506!16s%2Fg%2F11rb5dk6yf?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTEwNC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D). No code is necessary to open the gate.  


**Finding the Seminar Center**

The workshop will be held in in the Moonstone Seminar Center. Signs will be posted to guide you from the shuttle stop. The attached campus map indicates the precise location.
